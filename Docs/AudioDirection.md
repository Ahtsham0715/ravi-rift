# Ravi Rift Audio Direction

Ravi Rift needs premium fighting-game audio energy without copying any specific existing game, announcer, voice, sample, or copyrighted sound.

## Current Placeholder Pass

`Tools/generate_source_assets.py` now generates layered original PCM WAVs instead of one-note beeps:

- Impact layers: noise transient, sub thump, metallic/high spark.
- Block: metallic shield-like flash with short tail.
- UI: short tick, confirm and back tones.
- Big moments: wall splat, KO, counter hit, throw, rage ready and super riser.
- Announcer-style placeholders: round start, fight, KO and perfect as synthetic formant stingers.

These are still placeholder/generated source assets. Replace later with professionally recorded/mixed Foley and original announcer VO.

## Announcer Direction

The final announcer should be original: confident, modern, slightly cinematic, not an imitation of any existing fighting game announcer. Suggested line list:

- Round one

- Fight
- KO
- Perfect
- Counter hit

- Rage ready

- Hamza Kade wins

## Mix Targets

- Hits must cut through music and crowd noise.
- Blocks need less sub than clean hits but more metallic high-mid detail.
- Counter hits should have a crisp recognizer layer.
- Supers should use a longer riser and impact tail.
- Haptics should mirror the sound intensity but remain optional.
